# Gate 5 — Journal claim freeze

Status: **NOT STARTED**.

## Exit criteria

- All final protocols and seed counts frozen before statistical reruns.
- Final reruns completed without selective exclusion; exclusions documented.
- Aggregates, uncertainty, statistical tests, and negative results independently checked.
- Every claim maps to a table/figure and immutable experiment IDs.
- Conference-versus-journal difference table completed.
- At least 30% new material verified under the publisher's interpretation.
- Conference version cited; reused figures/tables carry source citations; no large verbatim reuse.
- New title, abstract, and conclusion completed.
- Limitations include instability, inference, geometry, counterfactual plausibility, dataset leakage/bias, and non-clinical status.
- Deadline and submission checklist reviewed by the senior developer and coauthors.

Claims are frozen only after final statistical reruns, not after exploratory results.
